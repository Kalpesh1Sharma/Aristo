- User-selected preference persists through localStorage.
- Apply the theme consistently across all application routes.
- Avoid hydration mismatches or visible theme flashes.
- Accessible toggle with descriptive labels and keyboard support.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Make light mode equally polished and readable.
- Do not introduce a large theme library unless necessary.
- Ensure the toggle is present in the shared navigation.
